# Join the team

GameSeek is early, small, and already in people's hands. If you want your work to show up in an app gamers actually open, there is a seat here. Lead, code, design, test, or look after the community.

## Why people join

- The product is still early, so your work shows up in it
- You can influence direction, not only tickets
- The group is small and direct
- You learn as the project grows

## Open roles

### Project lead

You keep the project moving and help decide what GameSeek becomes.

- Set direction and priority
- Coordinate development, design, community, and social work
- Represent GameSeek inside and outside the team
- Hold the whole picture while each group does its part

This role has real responsibility and real influence.

### Developer lead

You lead developers in one area and own the technical calls there.

- Coordinate people and review their work
- Set technical direction for the app, the website, the backend, or the frontend
- Keep the code consistent
- Mentor the people on your team

You should be comfortable building and leading.

### Design lead

You define how GameSeek looks and you lead design.

- Own design decisions for the app and the website
- Keep one visual language across both
- Review work and give useful feedback
- Sit with developers until a design is actually shipped

A strong eye for interface design on web and mobile helps.

### Community lead

You build the community and lead the people who support it.

- Plan community projects and events
- Guide the community support group
- Keep the space active and welcoming
- Carry community feedback back to the core team

### Developer

Pick the area you want to work in.

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Website | The GameSeek website |
| App | The GameSeek client |
| Backend | APIs, data, and server logic |
| Frontend | Interface components and layout |

The stack:

| Technology | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| React and TypeScript | The app interface |
| JavaScript | Scripting and site behavior |
| Python | Backend and tools |

You do not need every row. Apply for the part you know and enjoy.

### Designer

You shape screens and assets.

- Design interface screens for the app and the website
- Make layouts, icons, and illustrations
- Work with the design lead and with developers
- Improve how the product feels to use

Figma or Adobe XD experience helps. A clear eye matters more.

### Project support

You keep work organized.

- Collect ideas and feedback
- Help structure tasks
- Support the project lead on planning

### Social media

You grow the public presence of GameSeek.

- Create posts, video, and announcements
- Look after the brand in public
- Work with design and community on launches

### Community support

You are the first person a new member meets.

- Welcome people and answer questions
- Help keep the community friendly
- Support events run by the community lead
- Pass feedback and problems to the team

### Beta tester

You try builds before they are public.

You will:

- Use new features before release
- Report bugs and say what felt wrong
- Influence what ships

You get:

- Early access to versions and features
- A look at how the product is built
- A direct line to the people building it
- Credit as an early supporter

No engineering background is required.

## Before you write

This is a volunteer passion project. It is not a job and it is not paid.

- Joining is free
- There is no long-term commitment
- You can leave when you want
- Experience level is not a filter

## How to apply

Email **jobs@gameseekapp.com** with:

1. The role you want
2. A short introduction: who you are and what you like doing
3. Optional: experience, skills, or ideas you would bring

Every message is read by a person.
