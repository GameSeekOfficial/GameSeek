# Community rules

These rules keep GameSeek fair, safe, and pleasant for everyone. They apply to the app, the website, and every GameSeek community space.

## 1. Be respectful

Treat other people the way you want to be treated.

Insults, hate speech, harassment, and discrimination are not allowed.

## 2. Do not cheat or exploit

Cheats, hacks, and deliberate abuse of bugs are not allowed.

Breaking this rule leads to an immediate, permanent ban.

## 3. Do not spam or advertise

Unsolicited ads, spam, and mass messages are not allowed. Keep the conversation relevant to the people in the room.

## 4. Respect privacy

Do not share someone else's private or personal information unless they have clearly agreed. That includes names, contact details, and anything they did not post themselves.

## 5. Keep it appropriate for every age

Do not post explicit, offensive, or adults-only content. GameSeek is meant to stay usable for every age group.

## Consequences

A violation can lead to:

- a warning
- a temporary mute or other restriction
- a temporary or permanent ban

The response depends on what happened and whether it has happened before. GameSeek may act at its own discretion.

These rules exist so the community stays fair and welcoming. Thank you for being here.
