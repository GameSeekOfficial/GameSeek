# About GameSeek

GameSeek is an independent project. It is created and led by a solo developer who started it at 18, with a growing group of people helping around the edges.

The aim is a clean platform where players can find each other and grow a community without extra clutter in the way.

## Vision

GameSeek is for players who want the tool to stay out of the way.

- Connect with other players
- Find teammates who fit how you play
- Build a community without friction

The product should feel fast and familiar the first time you open it.

## Security and privacy

Safety of the community comes first.

To protect GameSeek from spam, abuse, and other threats, security systems may log and process technical data such as IP addresses. Those logs are used for platform protection, blacklist management, and automated moderation.

When you contact support, GameSeek may store your email address and the details you send so the team can answer you and manage the ticket.

GameSeek collects what it needs to run, secure, and improve the service. User data is not sold. The [security architecture](security/architecture.md) describes the technical layers. The [legal notice](legal/impressum.md) names the operator.
